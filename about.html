WHO WE ARE

About our department

We help learners understand technology and use it to solve meaningful problems.

OUR PURPOSE

Knowledge with practical impact.

The Department of Computing provides a learning environment where students can explore computer systems, software, data, and digital problem-solving. Our goal is to support learners as they develop the knowledge and confidence to contribute to the technology community.

Through a balance of theory, practical work, and collaboration, students are encouraged to keep learning and to approach challenges with curiosity and integrity.

OUR COMMITMENT

Learn. Collaborate. Create.

We value critical thinking, responsible technology use, teamwork, and continuous improvement.

OUR VALUES

What guides us

Curiosity

Ask questions, explore ideas, and keep learning.

Integrity

Work honestly and use technology responsibly.

Collaboration

Share knowledge and build better solutions together.
